The Big Three Lifts

1. Squat

Barbell ko back par rakh kar baithna aur wapas khade hona. Commands: "Squat" aur "Rack".

2. Bench Press

Bench par let kar barbell ko chest par touch karna aur press karna. Commands: "Start", "Press", aur "Rack".

3. Deadlift

Zameen se barbell ko utha kar seedha khada hona. Commands: "Down".

Essential Gear

  • Lifting Belt: Core ko tight rakhne aur spine ko support dene ke liye (10mm ya 13mm).
  • Knee Sleeves: Squat ke time knees ko garam rakhne aur joint support ke liye.
  • Wrist Wraps: Heavy Bench Press mein wrist ko straight rakhne ke liye.
